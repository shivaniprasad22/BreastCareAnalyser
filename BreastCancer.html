Breast Cancer Prediction


With 90,000 deaths per annum, tragically, a woman loses her life to breast cancer every eight minutes in the country.

Every four minutes, an Indian woman is diagnosed with breast cancer. One in every two women diagnosed with breast cancer, dies.

With being the most common type of cancer in women, breast cancer accounts for 14% of cancers in Indian women.
